# Kalimat Plus كَلِمَات

A free Fusha (Modern Standard Arabic) vocabulary trainer for discussing deeper topics. Live at https://kalimat-plus.ummahapps.ai/

**Words:** 302 words and phrases with full harakat in five topics: discussion language (opinions, contrast, cause and effect, linking words), religion and worship, tarbiyyah and upbringing, relationships and character, and the environment.

## Features
- Tap any word to hear it at slow, normal or natural speed (uses the device's Arabic text-to-speech voice)
- An example sentence with English translation for every entry
- Verbs with present tense and verbal noun; nouns with sound or broken plurals
- Notes on 74 words: which preposition a verb takes, tricky plurals and endings, and words often confused
- Games: read it, say it from memory, say the verb forms, say the plural
- Mark words as learned, then as known well
- Add your own words

## SEO and GEO
`index.html` has meta tags, Open Graph and JSON-LD (WebApplication + FAQPage). `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` sit at the root.

## Running locally

node serve.js
