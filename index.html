      About Kalimat Plus

      Questions

      What is Kalimat Plus?

      A free Fusha (Modern Standard Arabic) vocabulary trainer for learners who know the basics and want words for deeper conversations: religion and worship, tarbiyyah and upbringing, relationships and character, and the environment.

      Is Kalimat Plus free?

      Yes. It is free, needs no account, has no ads and runs in your web browser.

      Does Kalimat Plus teach tarbiyyah vocabulary?

      Yes. The Tarbiyyah and upbringing topic has 95 words and phrases about raising children in Islam: adab, role models, fitrah, values, encouragement and reward, discipline, rights and duties, and being dutiful to parents (birr al-walidayn). It also covers the difficulties of bringing up children today: smartphones and screen time, social media, influencers, video games and addiction, peer pressure, bullying, identity, mental health, privacy and parental controls. Each has an example sentence.

      How is it different from Kalimat?

      Kalimat teaches over 400 everyday beginner words. Kalimat Plus moves on to deeper topics such as faith, tarbiyyah, character and the environment, and adds example sentences for every entry, verb forms (present tense and verbal noun) and a game for saying the verb forms.

      How does it pronounce the Arabic?

      It uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. For the best pronunciation, add an Arabic voice in your phone or computer's speech or language settings, then choose it in the Voice menu.

      Where is my progress saved?

      Your learned and known-well words and your own words are saved in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

      Who is it for?

      Intermediate learners of Modern Standard Arabic, including GCSE and A-level students, home-schoolers, and adults in Quranic or Madinah-style Arabic classes who want to discuss ideas in Fusha.
